About us

Clear, practical, and on your side.

G. Omachi & Co. is a Nigerian law firm providing practical, responsive and commercially focused legal services to individuals, families, entrepreneurs, companies and organisations. Our approach is simple: understand the client, understand the problem and identify the most effective legal solution.

We have a long-standing background in advisory work, commercial transactions, regulatory matters, litigation and alternative dispute resolution, and today our practice is centred on: Corporate, Commercial & Regulatory; Family Law; and Immigration Law. Litigation and dispute resolution support those areas whenever court proceedings, negotiation or mediation become necessary.
