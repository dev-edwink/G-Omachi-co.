About us

Integrity, competence and advocacy.

We're a small, hands-on set of barristers and solicitors who've spent the last decade in courtrooms and boardrooms across Nigeria advising local businesses, multinationals and individuals, Nigerian and foreign, across every civil and criminal jurisdiction in the country.

Most of what we do is detail work: reviewing documents, negotiating terms, and turning up in court when a matter goes that far. We'd rather be known for doing that well, consistently, than for how we describe ourselves.
