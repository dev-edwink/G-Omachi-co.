Practical legal solutions.
Professional service. Trusted advice.

G. Omachi & Co. is a Nigerian law firm providing practical, responsive and commercially focused legal services to individuals, families, entrepreneurs, companies and organisations. Our approach is simple: understand the client, understand the problem and identify the most effective legal solution.

The firm has a long-standing background in legal advisory work, commercial transactions, regulatory matters, litigation and alternative dispute resolution. We have represented individuals, businesses and public-sector clients before courts and regulatory institutions across Nigeria.

Today we are building on that experience through a focused practice in three areas: Corporate, Commercial & Regulatory; Family Law; and Immigration Law. We combine traditional legal professionalism with modern communication, so clients can consult and work with our lawyers efficiently, including where physical attendance at our offices is unnecessary.

Our mission

To provide effective, practical and client-focused legal services efficiently and at a reasonable cost.

Our vision

To be a trusted modern law firm recognised for integrity, professionalism, responsiveness and excellence in client service.
