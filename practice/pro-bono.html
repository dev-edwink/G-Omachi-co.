Practice Area

Pro Bono

Legal support for individuals and causes who wouldn’t otherwise have access to it.

Not every matter that deserves proper legal representation comes with a budget for it. We take on a limited number of pro bono matters each year for individuals and causes we believe in.

If you think your situation might qualify, reach out and tell us about it, we can't take every case, but we do review every request.

How we can help

  • ✓Access-to-justice matters
  • ✓Support for individuals facing hardship
  • ✓Assistance to community causes
  • ✓Limited capacity, case-by-case basis
  • ✓Referrals where we’re unable to assist directly
